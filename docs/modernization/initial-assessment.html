Product and deployment facts

Faxbot runs on the customer's self-hosted/on-premises installation. Faxbot.net is the website, simulated demo and documentation; it is not the established production API. The iOS companion is in TestFlight and connects through a secured WireGuard, Tailscale or Cloudflare Tunnel path.

The user requires a finished product and complete RBAC. UI upgrades are allowed. DNS migration is lower priority and has not been performed. These points replace the initial report's unresolved deployment question and “keep the UI” framing.

Current architecture

Implemented runtime structure

Validated configuration revisions feed provider activation. Accepted faxes retain their provider/account configuration. Persisted delivery attempts, worker leases, callbacks and polling track accepted work.

Current identity and resource policy are connected to authentication, outbound operations and settings/provider access.

Incomplete integration

Management HTTP/UI, the cookie-based console, inbound resource routing and several privileged/client surfaces are not fully connected to the new access system.

Legacy API-key creation still produces keys without the new principal/binding state. Complete RBAC is not established.
